Project overview

This project explores how Salesforce can support a telecommunications business by organizing plans and sales opportunities, then automating parts of the sales process.

Key features

  • Custom objects and fields for telecom plan information.
  • Relationships between relevant CRM records.
  • Flow Builder automation for opportunity status changes.
  • Email alert concept when an opportunity reaches Closed Won, including relevant plan details.
  • Reports and dashboards to summarize CRM information.

What I learned: how data modeling, record-triggered flows, and reporting work together in a CRM.
